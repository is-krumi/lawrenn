Every missed call is a missed client. That's not a metaphor — it's a math problem.

The average law firm misses **42% of inbound calls**. When you factor in that a single personal injury matter can be worth $15,000–$50,000 in fees, the arithmetic gets uncomfortable fast. If your firm receives 50 calls a month and misses 21 of them, you're not just losing leads. You're leaving a significant portion of your potential annual revenue on the table — every single month.

## Why Firms Miss Calls

It's not negligence. It's the nature of legal work. Attorneys are in depositions, client meetings, court, or simply focused on the work that keeps existing clients happy. Receptionists have lunch breaks, sick days, and can only be in one conversation at a time.

The problem isn't that firms don't care about intake. It's that the current model — a human answering a phone — has hard limits.

## What Callers Actually Do

Research consistently shows that most legal consumers contact only one or two firms before making a decision. They don't leave detailed voicemails. They don't email. They call the next firm on the list.

The bar for losing a potential client is a single ring-through to voicemail.

## The Compounding Effect

Beyond the immediate revenue loss, missed calls create a compounding problem:

**1. No second chance.** Unlike B2B sales, legal consumers rarely circle back. The moment of need drives the call. If you miss it, someone else gets that client.

**2. Review damage.** Prospects who can't reach you sometimes leave negative reviews. "Called three times, never heard back" is a pattern that affects your Google rating and future intake.

**3. Skewed perception of demand.** When you only speak to a fraction of your potential clients, you may underestimate demand and under-invest in growth. Your pipeline looks smaller than it is.

## What the Best Firms Are Doing

The firms growing fastest aren't necessarily the largest or the most established. They're the ones that have made it structurally impossible to miss an intake opportunity.

That means:
- **24/7 coverage** — calls answered after hours, on weekends, on holidays
- **Immediate qualification** — the first conversation captures the key facts of the matter
- **Instant follow-up** — the attorney or intake coordinator has a structured summary ready within minutes of the call ending

Some firms achieve this with a dedicated intake team. Most can't justify the cost. AI-powered intake is closing that gap.
